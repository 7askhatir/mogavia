Entrance tickets, meals, local guides and personal expenses are paid separately. We can suggest a guide where one is useful, and we will tell you honestly if a site is not worth the detour on your schedule.

Questions before you book

How do I confirm a booking?

Send your request through the booking form, then confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Your journey is confirmed when we reply with the vehicle, meeting point and final price.

Is a deposit or online payment required?

No online payment is taken by this website. Payment is arranged directly with your driver; confirm the accepted method when you book.

What time do excursions leave?

Long trips start early to make the most of the day. We agree the exact pickup time with you the day before travel.

Can I request a child seat?

Tell us the ages of any children travelling and we will confirm the best available option before your journey.

Are entrance fees and meals included?

No. Tickets, meals and local guides are paid separately unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Can we combine two excursions?

Often, yes. Send us the places you would like to see and we will suggest a realistic route and price.

Have a different day in mind?

Combine two places, add a stop or build a multi-day route. Tell us what you would like to see.
