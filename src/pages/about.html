Who we are

Essaouira is home. Morocco is the route.

Taxi Tours Essaouira is a small local transfer service built around private vehicles and personal planning. We work with travellers arriving at Essaouira, Marrakech, Agadir and Casablanca airports, and with guests who want to see more of the country without joining a group tour.

Our drivers know the coast road north to Safi and El Jadida, the busy approaches to Marrakech, and the mountain passes towards Imlil and Ouarzazate. That local knowledge turns a long drive into a comfortable, well-paced day.

We keep the service simple: one clear quote, one private vehicle, and a driver who answers the phone. No online payment is taken on this website; you confirm the details with us before you travel.

How we work

Small enough to care about the details.

We are not a booking platform or a call centre. The people who answer your message are the people who arrange your driver — and we would rather do a few journeys well than promise more than we can deliver.

  • Clear communication

    We reply in English, French, Arabic or Spanish, and confirm every detail in writing before pickup.

  • Fair, fixed quotes

    The price is for the whole vehicle, not per person. Any extra stops or waiting time are agreed in advance.

  • Comfortable vehicles

    Air-conditioned sedans, minivans, vans and minibuses, cleaned and checked before each journey.

  • Local accountability

    You deal with the same local team from the first message to the final drop-off.
